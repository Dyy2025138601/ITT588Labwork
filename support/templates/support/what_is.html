What Is Domestic Violence and GBV?

Domestic violence is abuse by a family member or intimate partner to control or harm another person. Gender-Based Violence (GBV) is harm directed at a person because of their gender.

Hitting, slapping, choking, pushing, or any act that causes bodily harm.

Insults, threats, humiliation, isolation, and controlling behaviour.

Any sexual act without consent, including within marriage.

Controlling money, preventing work, or withholding financial support.
Abuse is never the victim's fault. Help is available.
